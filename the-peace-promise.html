A LITTLE ABOUT ASHLEY

Hey, I’m Ashley!

I’m a wife, mama to three girls, worship leader, and the woman behind The Peace Promise.

And while I wear a lot of hats, I can tell you that motherhood has been one of the most beautiful, stretching, rewarding, and downright exhausting parts of my life.

I love my family deeply. But somewhere between the schedules, the laundry, the appointments, the constant questions, and trying to remember what everybody needs (including myself), I realized something:

I was spending so much time managing our life that I couldn’t enjoy it.

And I didn’t want that to be our story.

I wanted more laughter. More patience. More connection. More moments where I could actually be present instead of mentally running through the next seventeen things on my to-do list.

Most importantly, I wanted to experience the peace I kept praying for — not just talk about it.

That’s partly why I created The Peace Promise.

Not because I’ve mastered motherhood or have some magical system that makes everything run perfectly. Trust me, we still live in a real house with real people and real laundry!

But because in my personal time with the Lord, He gave me fresh perspective. I believe we can love our families wholeheartedly without believing we have to carry everything ourselves.

I’m also the founder of Rooted Women Collective, where I create spaces, experiences, and resources that help women feel seen, supported, and encouraged as they navigate life’s different seasons.

My hope is that this workbook feels like a little of that same space for you.

A place to be honest. To breathe. To reflect. To invite God into the messy middle. And to begin making small, meaningful changes.

Because you deserve to enjoy the life you’re working so hard to hold together.

I’m glad you’re here.

With love,

Ashley
