INTRODUCING THE PEACE PROMISE

A practical place to begin again.

The Peace Promise is a 30-day guided reflection workbook created from Ashley’s experience, faith, and desire to enjoy the family God has given her. Through Scripture, reflection, practical tools, and small daily actions, you’ll explore how to recognize patterns, understand your reactions, communicate more intentionally, and reconsider how responsibility is shared at home.

It is not a promise that your family will change in 30 days. It is an invitation to begin practicing meaningful changes within your own circumstances.

MEET THE WOMAN BEHIND THE WORKBOOK

Hi, I’m Ashley.

Before we begin, I want you to know something.

I didn’t create The Peace Promise because I have motherhood all figured out. I created it because I know what it feels like to love your family deeply and still feel exhausted by everything it takes to care for them.

I’m Ashley Miller, a wife, mother of three daughters, worship leader, strategist, and founder of Rooted Women Collective.

I know the tension between being grateful for the life you have and feeling overwhelmed by the weight of caring for it.

I kept coming back to one thought:

“I want to enjoy the family God has given me, not spend every day exhausted by the responsibility of holding it together.”

This workbook grew from that desire. It isn’t a formula for a perfect home. It’s a space to reflect, pray, recognize patterns, and take practical steps toward change.

Through Rooted Women Collective, I create spaces where women can be honest about the seasons they’re navigating and find encouragement as they grow.

My hope is that these pages offer you a little of that same space.

You don’t have to have everything figured out to begin.

With love and grace,

Ashley Miller

A FEW THINGS YOU MAY BE WONDERING

Before you begin.

Adapt activities to your circumstances. Shared-responsibility exercises are not an obligation to negotiate with someone who is abusive or coercive.

Do I need to complete it in 30 consecutive days?

No. Move through the workbook at your own pace.

Is this only for married mothers?

No. Mothers parenting alone can adapt shared-responsibility activities to their circumstances.

Is this therapy or counseling?

No. It is a personal, Scripture-informed educational and reflection resource.

Can I use it with a church group?

Individual purchases are for personal use. Group use requires separate permission.

Will this change my family’s behavior?

No specific result is guaranteed. The workbook focuses on reflection, personal choices, communication, and practical next steps.
